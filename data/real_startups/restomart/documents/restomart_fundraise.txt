RESTOMART — FUNDRAISING INFORMATION

Investment Context
------------------
RestoMart has been considered as a potential angel investment.
The company is bootstrapped and has reported profitable operations.

Investment Considerations
-------------------------
Positive factors:
- Bootstrapped operating history
- Reported profitability
- More than 165 restaurant customers
- Revenue of approximately INR 2.68 crore
- Gross-margin improvement from approximately 3.1% to approximately 12%
- Approximately 95% fulfillment reliability
- Potential operating leverage from customer cluster density

Key Risks:
- Low-margin nature of food-supply operations
- Supply-chain execution risk
- Working-capital requirements
- Customer retention risk
- Customer concentration risk
- Competitive pressure
- Difficulty scaling micro-fulfillment economically
- Need to validate contribution margin
- Need to validate sustainability of gross-margin improvement

Required Diligence Before Investment
------------------------------------
The following should be obtained and independently validated:
1. Audited financial statements.
2. Monthly revenue for at least the last 24 months.
3. Gross-profit and contribution-margin calculation.
4. Bank statements.
5. Customer-level revenue concentration.
6. Customer retention and churn.
7. Order frequency.
8. Average order value.
9. CAC and customer acquisition channels.
10. Working-capital cycle.
11. Inventory ageing.
12. Wastage data.
13. Detailed founder/shareholder cap table.
14. Current liabilities and debt.
15. Formal fundraising documentation.
16. Current valuation and transaction terms.

Investment View
--------------
The available information is sufficiently interesting for further diligence but should
not by itself be treated as sufficient evidence for an investment decision.

The most important validation is whether reported revenue growth, profitability, and
gross-margin improvement translate into sustainable contribution economics at scale.
