RESTOMART — INVESTOR BRIEF

Company
-------

RestoMart is a B2B food-supply and restaurant technology company
focused on simplifying procurement for restaurants.

The company operates a curated procurement marketplace combined
with supply-chain and fulfillment operations.

The business focuses on improving procurement reliability,
availability, delivery speed, and operating efficiency for
restaurant customers.

Product and Operating Model
---------------------------

RestoMart provides restaurants with a curated selection of
food-supply products and procurement services.

The operating model is based on:
- Curated SKUs
- Cluster-based restaurant density
- Micro-fulfillment centers
- Rapid delivery
- Supply-chain coordination
- Technology-enabled procurement

The target delivery proposition discussed for the business is
approximately four-hour delivery.

Customers and Traction
----------------------

RestoMart has more than 165 restaurant customers.

The company has demonstrated repeat usage from restaurant
customers and operates a B2B model rather than a consumer
marketplace model.

Reported fulfillment reliability is approximately 95%.

Financial Performance
---------------------

RestoMart has reported approximately INR 2.68 crore of revenue.

The company has operated as a bootstrapped business and has
reported profitable operations.

Gross margin has improved materially during the operating period,
from approximately 3.1% to approximately 12%.

Investment Context
------------------

The company has been evaluated as a potential angel investment.

The previously discussed valuation cap was approximately INR 9.25 crore.

The current fundraising amount is INR 2 crore.

The minimum investment amount discussed was INR 2 lakh.

The company is bootstrapped and profitable based on the information reviewed.

Key Investment Questions
------------------------

Important areas for further diligence include:
1. Sustainability of the reported gross-margin improvement.
2. Restaurant retention and repeat-order behavior.
3. Revenue concentration among major restaurant customers.
4. Customer acquisition cost.
5. Contribution margin after fulfillment and delivery costs.
6. Procurement working-capital requirements.
7. Inventory and wastage economics.
8. Scalability of cluster-based micro-fulfillment.
9. Competitive differentiation versus restaurant procurement alternatives.
10. Whether the current valuation appropriately reflects the growth opportunity and operating risks.
