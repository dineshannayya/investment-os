# Investment OS

## README \(Expanded\)

Version: 1\.0  
Project: Investment OS  


## 1\. Welcome

Investment OS is an AI\-native investment platform that combines traditional investment management with artificial intelligence to support startup evaluation, due diligence, portfolio management, market intelligence, and investment committee workflows\.

## 2\. Vision

Build an enterprise\-grade AI operating system for venture capital, angel investors, family offices, accelerators, and corporate innovation teams\. The platform assists—not replaces—human decision makers by combining structured data, documents, and AI reasoning\.

## 3\. Problem Statement

Most investment workflows are fragmented across spreadsheets, email, shared drives, presentation decks, messaging applications, and disconnected databases\. Investment OS centralizes data, automates repetitive analysis, and provides AI\-assisted insights\.

## 4\. Project Goals

• AI\-assisted Due Diligence  
• Founder Intelligence  
• Financial Analysis  
• Technology Assessment  
• Legal Review  
• Portfolio Analytics  
• Knowledge Graph  
• Multi\-Agent AI Platform  
• Enterprise Governance

## 5\. Core Capabilities

Authentication, Organizations, Users, Startups, Founders, Investments, Portfolio Management, Documents, Research, AI Agents, Knowledge Graph, Administration\.

## 6\. High\-Level Architecture

User → Web/API → Services → AI Orchestrator → AI Agents → Knowledge Graph → Foundation Models → PostgreSQL/Document Store\.
